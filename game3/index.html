1 · Swap & Match

Tap a tile, then tap a neighbour — or swipe. Match 3 or more of the same creature-feature and every tile that's about to go lights up with a gold ring and glow first — so you can see the whole match, including any beam or bomb chain, before it pops. Tiles fall, new ones drop in, and chain reactions pay out bigger every time.

2 · Make the Specials

Match 4 and you forge a Projector Beam — match it again (or double-tap it) and it blasts its whole row or column. Match 5 for an Atomic Popcorn Bomb that clears everything around it.

3 · Beat the Showing

Hit the score target and finish the collection goals before your moves run out. There are 500 showings across 50 reels — and the tiles you need are boosted: extra copies of every collection-goal tile are threaded into the reel, so the ones you need turn up far more often. A 7th tile joins the cast from Showing 3, a third collection goal arrives at Showing 21, and from Showing 8 the house exclusive appears: the Static Veil, a translucent sheet of haunted TV static clinging to board squares. Clear tiles on a veiled square to burn the static off that square — and a showing with static on it isn't beaten until every last wisp is gone. From Showing 31 some squares wear Dense Static that takes two hits.
